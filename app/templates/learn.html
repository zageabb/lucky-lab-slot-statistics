CLASSROOM NOTES

Read the result, not a story into it.

Plain-language foundations for probability experiments.

01

Return to player

RTP is the expected payout per unit wagered over a very large number of independent spins. A 96% RTP does not promise 96 credits back from each 100-credit session.

02

Hit rate

Hit rate is the share of spins returning anything. A return can still be smaller than its stake, so a hit is not necessarily a profitable spin.

03

Volatility

Standard deviation describes spread around expected return. Models with similar RTP can feel very different when one pays little and often while another pays rarely and more.

04

Re-wagering

Cumulative wagered measures every stake, including credits previously returned. It can exceed the original allocation without any additional allocation.

05

Sample size

Larger samples usually bring observed RTP closer to theoretical RTP, but convergence is noisy. Confidence intervals describe uncertainty, not guarantees.

06

Gambler’s fallacy

After a losing streak, an independent next spin has unchanged probabilities. No result is “due,” and increasing stake does not improve the odds.
